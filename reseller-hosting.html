Reseller Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about starting and scaling your reseller business.

What is reseller hosting and how do I make money with it?

Reseller hosting allows you to buy server storage and bandwidth in bulk at wholesale prices, and then divide it into individual hosting packages that you sell to your clients under your own brand, setting your own prices and keeping 100% of the profits.

Will my clients know that Host Divine is the upstream provider?

No. Our reseller platform is 100% white label. You use private nameservers (e.g., ns1.yourbrand.com), your brand logo on the cPanel control panel, and your own billing and contact links. Host Divine is completely invisible.

What is the difference between cPanel Reseller and Master Reseller?

A standard cPanel Reseller can only create and manage end-user cPanel accounts. A Master Reseller has the elevated capability to create and sell both end-user cPanel accounts AND new WHM Reseller accounts to other people who want to become resellers.

Is overselling enabled on all reseller packages?

Yes! Overselling is fully enabled across all our reseller tiers. You can allocate 10 GB to each of 10 clients even if you only have 50 GB total, as long as the combined actual usage does not exceed your plan's storage limit.
