General

General & Billing Questions

What makes Host Divine different from other hosting companies?

Host Divine has been operating high-availability hosting infrastructure since 2004. Unlike low-cost oversold hosts, every Host Divine shared and reseller server is powered by pure NVMe enterprise SSDs, LiteSpeed Web Server with LSCache, CloudLinux cagefs isolation, and real-time Imunify360 AI malware filtering. Furthermore, we maintain a dedicated 24/7/365 engineering desk with guaranteed responses under 15 minutes.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept all major international payment gateways including Visa, MasterCard, American Express, PayPal, bank wire transfers, and leading local gateways in Bangladesh (bKash, Nagad, Rocket, DBBL Nexus) for convenient .BD domain and hosting billing.

Do you offer a money-back guarantee?

Yes. All shared cPanel hosting and reseller hosting accounts come with an unconditional 30-day money-back guarantee. If you are unsatisfied for any reason, submit a cancellation request within 30 days for a full refund of your hosting fees. Please note that domain registrations, dedicated servers, and software licenses are non-refundable per vendor policies.

How fast is my hosting account activated after payment?

Account activation is completely automated. Once your payment clears through our secure gateway, your cPanel or WHM credentials, nameservers, and server IP details will be dispatched to your registered email address within 60 seconds.

cPanel Hosting

Web Hosting & Technical Capabilities

What control panel do I get with shared web hosting?

You receive the authentic, fully licensed cPanel control panel with full privileges. It features the modern Jupiter interface, Softaculous 1-click app installer (for WordPress, Joomla, Laravel, PrestaShop), full phpMyAdmin access, DNS zone editor, unlimited email forwarders, autoresponders, and cron job management.

Can I switch PHP versions and customize PHP extensions?

Yes. With our integrated CloudLinux PHP Selector, you can select any version from PHP 7.4 through PHP 8.0, 8.1, 8.2, and 8.3 per domain or subdomain. You can also toggle over 80 extensions (including Redis, Memcached, ionCube, Imagick, OPcache) and adjust resource values such as upload_max_filesize and memory_limit up to 1024MB directly from cPanel.

Are SSL certificates free and automatically renewed?

Yes. AutoSSL is active on every Host Divine web hosting server. As soon as your domain points to our nameservers, a free Let's Encrypt 256-bit SSL certificate is automatically issued and will renew seamlessly in the background without any manual action required.

How do your automated backups work?

We deploy JetBackup 5 on all production clusters. Snapshots of your web files, MySQL databases, email mailboxes, and DNS records are captured daily and stored on independent remote backup nodes. You can restore single files, tables, or complete accounts directly from your cPanel dashboard at any time.

Reseller Hosting

Reseller & Multi-Tier Infrastructure

What is the difference between Reseller, Master Reseller, and Alpha Reseller?

Standard Reseller hosting allows you to create independent cPanel accounts for your clients through WebHost Manager (WHM). Master Reseller introduces secondary privileges, allowing you to create standard Reseller accounts in addition to cPanel accounts. Alpha Reseller sits at the top of the hierarchy, empowering you to create Alpha Resellers, Master Resellers, and standard WHM Resellers—ideal for ambitious web agencies and hosting startups.

Are reseller accounts 100% white-label?

Yes, completely white-label. Host Divine branding is entirely concealed. You can configure your own custom vanity nameservers (such as ns1.yourbrand.com and ns2.yourbrand.com), upload your company logo inside cPanel, and provide your clients with your own branded webmail and billing URLs.

Can I integrate WHMCS or another billing platform?

Yes! Every reseller account provides full API access (WHM API tokens), allowing seamless 1-click provisioning and suspension via WHMCS, ClientExec, Blesta, or custom automation scripts.

Domain Registry

Domain Names & Official .BD Registration

What is required to register an official .BD domain?

.BD domains (such as .com.bd, .net.bd, .org.bd, .edu.bd, or direct .bd) are regulated by the Bangladesh Telecommunications Company Limited (BTCL). For commercial registrations, an updated Trade License or Certificate of Incorporation is required. For personal domains, a National ID (NID) or Passport copy must be submitted. All .BD domains are registered on a minimum 2-year government cycle.

How long does .BD domain approval take?

Once you submit your application and required documents through Host Divine, our administrative liaison verifies the paperwork and lodges it into the BTCL registry queue. Typical government verification and DNS delegation takes between 24 and 72 business hours.

Do I get full DNS management with my domain?

Yes. Every domain registered with Host Divine includes our redundant Anycast DNS management console. You can configure A, AAAA, CNAME, MX, TXT (SPF/DKIM), and SRV records with instant propagation across global root servers.

Cloud Infrastructure

VPS & Dedicated Bare Metal Servers

Do I receive full root SSH access?

Yes. Both VPS and dedicated servers are delivered with 100% root SSH access (or Administrator Remote Desktop for Windows). You have complete freedom to install custom kernel modules, Docker, Kubernetes, database clusters, or proprietary control panels.

What virtualization technology powers your VPS servers?

All Host Divine VPS servers are built on pure KVM (Kernel-based Virtual Machine) hardware virtualization. KVM guarantees strict isolation of CPU cores, dedicated RAM allocations, and raw NVMe disk throughput without noisy-neighbor interference.

Is DDoS mitigation enabled by default?

Yes. All server traffic flows through our inline hardware scrubbing layer capable of filtering up to 10+ Tbps of malicious volumetric attack traffic (including SYN floods, UDP amplification, and HTTP layer-7 attacks) without latency degradation.
