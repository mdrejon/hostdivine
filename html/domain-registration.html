What happens right after I register a domain?

Your domain is registered in real time. You can manage nameservers, DNS records and forwarding straight away from the Robot client area.

Why are prices shown in BDT?

Domain payments are processed in Bangladeshi Taka. You can still pay by international card — your bank converts the amount.

Can I transfer my domain away later?

Yes. After the 60-day ICANN lock you can unlock the domain and request the EPP code from your dashboard at any time.

Do you offer .bd domains?

Yes. We handle official BTCL .bd registration including paperwork. See the .BD Domain page for details.
