Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear details on .BD domain rules, renewals and document requirements.

Why is the minimum registration period 2 years?

BTCL (Bangladesh Telecommunications Company Limited), the government registry for the .bd country code, enforces a mandatory minimum term of 2 years for all new registrations and subsequent renewals.

Can an individual person register a .com.bd domain without a company?

Yes. Individuals can register .com.bd or direct .bd domains using their government-issued National ID (NID) or Passport copy. For business names that are already registered trademarks, a Trade License is required.

Can foreign companies register a .bd domain?

Foreign companies can register .bd domains if they have a registered trademark or local office in Bangladesh, or by appointing a local administrative contact. Contact our compliance desk for custom foreign registration assistance.

How do I renew my existing .bd domain?

You can renew existing .bd domains through our portal even if originally registered elsewhere. Simply submit your domain name and BTCL registration ID, and we will process the BTCL renewal fees seamlessly.
