Before You Transfer

Transfer checklist

A few things to check at your current registrar so the transfer goes through first time.

  • The domain is at least 60 days old
  • Registrar lock is turned off
  • WHOIS privacy is disabled during transfer
  • You have the EPP / transfer auth code
  • The registrant contact stays the same during transfer

What is an EPP code?

An EPP code (also called an auth code or transfer secret) is a password your current registrar gives you. It proves you own the domain and authorizes the transfer.

  • For .uk domains, change the IPS tag instead of using a code.
  • Transfer prices are listed on the registration page.

Will my website or email go down during the transfer?

No. Keep your current nameservers during checkout and your website and email keep working throughout.

What is an EPP or authorization code?

A unique password from your current registrar that proves you own the domain and allows the transfer.

Does my remaining registration time carry over?

Yes, for gTLDs such as .com, .net and .org the remaining time carries over and a renewal year is added.

Can I transfer a domain registered less than 60 days ago?

No. ICANN rules block transfers within 60 days of registration or a previous transfer.
