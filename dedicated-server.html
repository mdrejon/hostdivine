Dedicated Server FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Key information regarding deployment, hardware and networking.

How quickly will my dedicated server be deployed?

Standard inventory models (like the DS-1 and DS-2) are typically racked, provisioned, and online within 4 to 12 hours. Custom hardware configurations with specialized RAID cards or multi-terabyte NVMe arrays are delivered within 24 to 48 hours.

Do I receive full IPMI / iDRAC out-of-band management?

Yes! Every server is assigned a dedicated, secure IPMI / iDRAC network port. You can view the server's screen, mount remote ISO images, access BIOS settings, and power cycle the machine anytime via your browser.

Can I order additional IPv4 subnets?

Yes, additional IPv4 subnets (/29, /28, /27, /26, /25, /24) are available with legitimate ARIN/RIPE justification (e.g. for virtual machines, SSL certificates, or hosting clients).

What happens if a hardware component fails?

We provide a 4-hour hardware replacement guarantee. Our datacenters maintain on-site spare parts inventories including hot-swap enterprise NVMe drives, RAM DIMMs, power supplies, and motherboards ready for immediate dispatch.

Need a custom enterprise bare metal configuration?

Our infrastructure architects can design custom multi-node clusters, private 10 Gbps SAN storage networks, and hybrid cloud topologies.
