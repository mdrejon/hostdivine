Registration FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful answers about purchasing and managing your domains.

What happens immediately after I register a domain?

Your domain is registered in real-time with the registry. You immediately receive full DNS management capabilities in your Robot Client Area where you can configure nameservers, email forwarding, or point it to your web hosting account.

Can I transfer my domain to another provider in the future?

Yes. Under ICANN rules, you can transfer your domain to any registrar after the standard 60-day initial registration lock. You can unlock your domain and request your EPP/Auth code anytime directly from your dashboard.

Why is WHOIS Privacy Protection recommended?

When you register a domain, ICANN requires contact details (name, phone, address, email) to be registered in the public WHOIS registry. WHOIS Privacy replaces your personal data with anonymous proxy data, preventing spam and identity harvesting.

Do you offer Bangladeshi .BD domain registration?

Yes! We provide official BTCL .bd and .com.bd domain registration with full assistance in document verification. Visit our dedicated .BD Domain Registration page for full details.
