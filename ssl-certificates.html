Isn't free SSL enough?

Free AutoSSL is great for blogs and small sites. Paid certificates add warranties, site seals, wildcard coverage and business validation that customers trust.

What is the difference between DV, OV and EV?

DV checks domain ownership only. OV also verifies your organization. EV adds the strictest business checks for the highest trust.

Do you install the certificate for me?

Yes. Installation is free on Host Divine hosting and we can guide you on any other server.

What does wildcard cover?

A wildcard certificate secures your domain and every first-level subdomain, such as shop.example.com and mail.example.com.
