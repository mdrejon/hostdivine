Guiding Principles

The principles that drive our engineering

We don't cut corners on hardware or customer care. Here is what makes Host Divine different.

Pure NVMe Architecture

We refuse to use slow spinning disks or legacy shared SATA drives for web workloads. Every cluster runs on PCIe NVMe SSDs in RAID-10 configuration.

Honest & Transparent Billing

No predatory renewal rate hikes or hidden setup charges. What you see on our pricing table is what you pay, with clear renewal costs displayed upfront.

Engineers, Not Chatbots

When you contact our support desk, you interact directly with certified Linux and Windows sysadmins capable of solving complex technical server challenges.

Proactive Security

From Imunify360 AI malware defense and CloudLinux CageFS account sandboxing to 1 Tbps DDoS mitigation, security is baked into our core.

Designed for Scale

Start with a $4.15/yr starter cPanel package and scale seamlessly to multi-tier Master Reseller accounts, KVM cloud nodes, and bare metal clusters.

Automated Daily Backups

Data integrity is sacred. We maintain off-site, point-in-time automated snapshots powered by JetBackup with self-service 1-click restore.

Milestones

Host Divine Timeline

A track record of technological innovation and uninterrupted service since 2004.

2004 — Company Established

Inception & First Server Cluster

Host Divine was founded under the ZELT technology group with a clear mission: delivering enterprise-grade web hosting with personal, dedicated customer support. Racked our first cPanel and dedicated server clusters.

2010 — Global Expansion

Multi-Tier Reseller & European Datacenters

Expanded infrastructure footprint into top-tier carrier-neutral facilities across the United States and Europe. Introduced multi-tier Master Reseller and Alpha Reseller architectures for web agencies.

2016 — Cloud Modernization

Pure SSD Storage & CloudLinux Isolation

Migrated 100% of our hosting fleet to enterprise SSD storage. Implemented CloudLinux OS and CageFS container virtualization across all shared and reseller servers, eliminating noisy-neighbour issues.

2021 — Enterprise NVMe & AI Security

LiteSpeed Enterprise & Imunify360 Layer

Adopted LiteSpeed Web Server across the entire platform, providing up to 20x faster page load times. Deployed Imunify360 real-time AI security defense and offsite JetBackup snapshot automation.

2026 — Present Day

Powering 240,000+ Websites Worldwide

Today, Host Divine delivers mission-critical hosting, KVM Cloud VPS, bare metal dedicated servers, and ccTLD domain management to thousands of developers, agencies, and businesses across 80+ countries.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about our company, network and operations.

Where are Host Divine servers physically located?

We operate enterprise server hardware in Tier-3+ carrier-neutral datacenters located in North America (Dallas, Chicago, New York), Western Europe (Frankfurt, London), and Asia / Middle East (Singapore, UAE), ensuring minimal latency worldwide.

What is the relationship between Host Divine and ZELT?

Host Divine is a specialized brand label and cloud infrastructure division of ZELT, registered in the United Arab Emirates. ZELT oversees the company's enterprise datacenter leases, global peering agreements, and technical engineering operations.

Do you provide a money-back guarantee?

Yes. All our shared web hosting, reseller hosting, and Windows hosting plans carry a 30-day risk-free money-back guarantee. If you are not completely satisfied, you can cancel within 30 days for a full refund of your hosting fees.
