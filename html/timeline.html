Milestones

Host Divine timeline

Uninterrupted service since 2004.

  1. 2004 — Company Established

    Inception & first server cluster

    Host Divine was founded under the ZELT technology group with one mission: enterprise-grade web hosting with personal, dedicated support. We racked our first cPanel and dedicated server clusters.

  2. 2010 — Global Expansion

    Multi-tier reseller & European datacenters

    Expanded into carrier-neutral facilities across the United States and Europe, and introduced Master and Alpha Reseller platforms for web agencies.

  3. 2016 — Cloud Modernization

    Pure SSD storage & CloudLinux isolation

    Moved 100% of the hosting fleet to enterprise SSD storage and deployed CloudLinux with CageFS on every shared and reseller server.

  4. 2021 — Enterprise NVMe & AI Security

    LiteSpeed Enterprise & Imunify360

    Adopted LiteSpeed Web Server platform-wide for up to 20x faster page loads, and deployed Imunify360 AI security with off-site JetBackup snapshots.

  5. 2026 — Present Day

    Powering 240,000+ websites

    Today Host Divine delivers hosting, KVM cloud VPS, bare metal servers and ccTLD domain management to developers, agencies and businesses in 80+ countries.
