What is reseller hosting and how do I make money with it?

You buy server space and bandwidth in bulk, split it into hosting packages and sell them to your clients under your own brand and prices. You keep 100% of the profit.

Will my clients know that Host Divine is the provider?

No. The platform is fully white-label: private nameservers, your logo on cPanel and your own billing and support links.

What is the difference between Reseller, Master and Alpha Reseller?

A cPanel Reseller creates cPanel accounts. A Master Reseller can also create and sell WHM reseller accounts. An Alpha Reseller sits at the top and can create Master Resellers, Resellers and cPanel accounts.

Are the prices monthly?

Yes. All reseller plans are billed monthly in USD. Setup is free on every plan.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. Upgrade between plans or move to Master or Alpha Reseller at any time without downtime.
