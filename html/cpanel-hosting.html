Contact support
Which control panel do I get?

You get the fully licensed cPanel control panel with the modern Jupiter interface, Softaculous 1-click installer, phpMyAdmin, DNS zone editor, email tools and cron jobs.

Can I switch PHP versions?

Yes. The CloudLinux PHP Selector lets you choose PHP 7.4 to 8.3 per domain and toggle more than 80 extensions such as Redis, ionCube, Imagick and OPcache.

Is SSL free and renewed automatically?

Yes. AutoSSL issues a free certificate as soon as your domain points to our nameservers and renews it in the background.

Will you migrate my existing website?

Yes. Our engineers move your files, databases and email from your old host for free, with zero downtime.

Are the prices yearly?

Yes. All Gold, Platinum and Advanced web hosting plans are billed once per year. The price shown is the full yearly price.
