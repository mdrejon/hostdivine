Transfer FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about transferring and renewing your domain names.

Will my website or business email go down during the transfer?

No. During the transfer checkout, you can choose to retain your current nameservers. Because nameserver delegation remains intact, your website, web hosting, and email routing experience zero downtime.

What is an EPP code or Authorization code?

An EPP code (also known as an Auth-Code or Transfer Secret) is a unique security password assigned to your domain by the registrar. It ensures that only the verified domain owner can authorize an external transfer.

Does my existing time left on the domain carry over?

Yes! For all gTLDs like .com, .net, and .org, ICANN regulations state that any remaining registration time carries over completely, and 1 additional year is added onto your existing expiration date upon transfer completion.

Can I transfer a domain registered less than 60 days ago?

Under ICANN global regulations, a domain cannot be transferred between registrars within 60 days of initial registration or a previous transfer. Once the 60-day window has passed, it can be transferred freely.
