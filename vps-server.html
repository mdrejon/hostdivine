Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common inquiries regarding our Cloud VPS servers.

How fast is my VPS provisioned after payment?

Our cloud orchestration system provisions and bootstraps your VPS automatically in less than 60 seconds. You will receive an email with your dedicated IP, SSH port, and root credentials immediately upon invoice completion.

Can I upgrade my VPS CPU, RAM, or storage later?

Yes. You can scale your VPS resources seamlessly at any time from your Robot client panel. Upgrades require only a brief reboot to resize the virtual disk and reallocate CPU/RAM.

Do you provide root access to the VPS?

Yes, every instance includes unrestricted root access via SSH on Linux or Administrator RDP access on Windows. You maintain total administrative sovereignty over your operating system.

Can I install custom control panels like cPanel, CyberPanel, or Docker?

Absolutely! You can install Docker, Kubernetes, cPanel/WHM, Plesk, CyberPanel, aaPanel, or configure raw stacks with Nginx and PostgreSQL without any restrictions.
