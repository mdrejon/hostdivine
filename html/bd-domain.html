Registration Procedure

How .bd registration works

BTCL processes every application. We prepare and track it for you.

  1. 01

    Submit application

    Authorization letter, National ID copy and 2 passport-size photos go to BTCL.

  2. 02

    Demand letter

    BTCL accepts the application and issues a demand letter for the registration.

  3. 03

    Payment

    The fee is deposited at the bank and the payment proof is submitted to BTCL.

  4. 04

    Activation

    BTCL activates the domain within 2 days of receiving payment confirmation.

Documents

What you need to register

Gather these documents before ordering. Our team formats the official BTCL application and agreement forms for you.

  • Authorization letter (company pad for organisations)
  • Photocopy of National ID (NID)
  • 2 passport-size photographs
  • Trade licence for company names
  • Eligibility proof for .gov.bd, .edu.bd and .ac.bd

Who maintains the .bd domain?

The .bd ccTLD is administered by the Ministry of Post and Telecommunications, and registrations are maintained by BTCL.

Can foreigners register a .bd domain?

Yes. .bd registration is open to anyone worldwide.

How long does activation take?

About 4 working days after you submit the order. BTCL activates within 2 days of receiving payment confirmation.

Why is a 2-year term recommended?

Renewing through BTCL takes time, so a longer term reduces the risk of accidental expiry.
