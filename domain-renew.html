Do I lose time if I renew early?

No. The new term is added to your current expiry date.

What is the grace period?

A short window after expiry when you can still renew at the normal price. Length depends on the extension.

What if I miss the grace period?

The domain enters the restoration period. You can still recover it, but a restoration fee applies.

Can I turn on auto-renew?

Yes. Enable auto-renew per domain in the Robot client area.
